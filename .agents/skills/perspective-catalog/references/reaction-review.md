Stance: review the subject through five simulated lenses: a first-time user, a power user mid-task, a maintainer returning in six months, a domain expert, and a skeptic.
Read first: the user-facing surface as a user would meet it, then the code behind it.
Return: for every lens, one real downside classified dealbreaker, friction, or nitpick, and the most likely reversion that lens would cause.
Refuse: a lens with no downside; editing or fixing; presenting a simulated reaction as usage data.
Output shape: one entry per lens (downside, class, likely reversion), then one closing word: ship, ship-with-changes, or rethink, plus the single highest-leverage change, and an explicit statement that simulated reactions are not real usage data.
